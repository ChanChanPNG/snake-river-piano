ROOTED IN OUR COMMUNITY

For Blackfoot & Snake River families.

Snake River Piano is located in Blackfoot, Idaho, and welcomes children from the Blackfoot and Snake River School Districts. Piano Lab is a convenient option for families looking for a music activity after the school day.

Homeschool families are welcome, too. Morning Piano Lab classes offer a way to include music in the school week.

YOUR PIANO TEACHER

Hi, I’m Diana Williams.

“I have a gentle approach that brings great results and I try my very best to help each student feel good about themselves.”

I’ve been teaching piano professionally since 1992. I have a Bachelor of Arts degree from Idaho State University and am a certified elementary education teacher. Teaching has been an important part of my life for more than 30 years.

My lessons focus on technique and sight-reading from the start, but I also want students to enjoy the process. I use different teaching materials and activities depending on a student’s age, experience, and goals.
