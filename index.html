THE HEART BEHIND THE MUSIC

Meet Diana Williams.

“I have a gentle approach that brings great results and I try my very best to help each student feel good about themselves.”

Diana began teaching piano professionally in 1992. With a Bachelor of Arts degree from Idaho State University and a background as a certified elementary education teacher, she brings deep experience and a warm, organized approach to every lesson.

She loves teaching students of all ages and abilities, using creative programs that keep lessons engaging while building the skills students need to become independent musicians.
